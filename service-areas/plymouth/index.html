Where we work in Plymouth

Plymouth questions

Things Plymouth clients usually ask.

Do you cover both the City of Plymouth and Plymouth Township?

Both. Our weekly routes cover the Old Village, downtown Plymouth, and the larger township subdivisions out toward Sheldon and Beck. Plymouth-Canton Community Schools families across all of those neighborhoods are on our route.

Can you work on small Old Village lots without damaging the property next door?

Yes. We size equipment to the access, not the other way around. For tighter Old Village properties that means smaller mowers, hand-edging where needed, and crew leads who know how to lay down protection between properties.

Do you do front bed redesigns for downtown Plymouth homes?

Often. Plymouth front beds are a high-leverage project because the whole street sees them. We will walk the property, talk through what you want the front of the house to feel like, and send a planting plan and bed redesign with detailed pricing.

How does fall leaf cleanup work for Plymouth's older streets?

For most Plymouth properties we run two passes in the fall, because the mature street trees drop in waves. That keeps the property looking right through November rather than buried in leaves while you wait for one big cleanup.
