Why Novi calls us

Larger lots. Higher expectations. Pavers everywhere. Novi is the city where details have to land or they stand out.

Novi has a different rhythm than the older inner-ring suburbs. Lots are larger, construction is newer, and the property standards are sharper. The homes in Bellagio, Tuscany Reserve, Vistas of Novi, and Maybury Park Estates were built around the assumption that the landscaping is going to look the part of the architecture. When it doesn't, the whole street notices.

That's most of what gets us called into Novi. Owners who built or bought a home with serious bones, and want a landscape company that runs at the same standard as the house. We work alongside Novi Community Schools families and parts of Northville Public, around Maybury State Park, and through the corridor along Beck Road and Grand River where so much of the new growth lives.

What that looks like on the ground: tighter mowing patterns on bigger lawns, paver driveway repairs and re-leveling, full hardscape builds with seat walls and outdoor kitchens, and planting designs that hold up to the visibility of corner lots and cul-de-sac entries. The property either looks intentional or it looks neglected. We make it look intentional.

And because so much of Novi sits on heavier clay with sub-grade water issues, we pay particular attention to drainage. Most of the patio failures we get called in to fix were built without it.

Novi properties tend to be larger lots with paver driveways, mature plantings, and higher-end hardscape, so weekly maintenance and project pricing reflects the scope, not a city premium. We give you a detailed line-item proposal so you see exactly what's driving the number.

Yes. Same crew handles spring through fall maintenance, hardscape and design installs, and winter snow and ice management for Novi residences and commercial properties along Grand River and Twelve Mile.
