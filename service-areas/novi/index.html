Novi questions

Things Novi clients usually ask.

Do you handle large Novi lots and estate properties?

Yes. A meaningful chunk of our weekly route in Novi is on lots over a half-acre, and we have several full-acre and multi-acre properties on contract. Our equipment and crew are scaled for that.

What does a paver patio cost in Novi?

For most Novi homes, paver patios run from about $15,000 on the small end up past $50,000 once you start adding seat walls, fire features, lighting, or covered structures. We provide free on-site estimates with detailed line-item pricing so you can see exactly what drives the number.

Do you work with Novi HOAs and subdivision boards?

Yes. We service common areas and entrance landscapes for several Novi subdivisions, and we are used to providing certificates of insurance and proper documentation that HOA boards expect.

How does snow removal work for Novi's longer driveways?

We bid each Novi driveway based on length, surface, and access. Most residential routes get cleared within a few hours of snowfall ending. Commercial properties get pre-treated and cleared on a tighter response window so businesses can open on time.
