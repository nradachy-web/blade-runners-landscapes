Why Northville calls us

Northville is really three different towns layered on top of each other. We work in all of them.

Northville has a personality you don't get anywhere else on the west side. The historic downtown district feels like a small Michigan town that time treated kindly: brick storefronts, shaded sidewalks, century-old homes with original limestone walks. A few minutes away, you're in luxury subdivisions like Northville Hills, Stonewater, and Country Club Village where the standards lean estate. Push west into Northville Township and the lots open up, the wooded subdivisions on Six Mile and Seven Mile take over, and the work gets bigger again.

Each one gets handled differently. The historic district needs smaller equipment, careful trim work around heritage plantings, and a planting palette that respects the architecture. The newer subdivisions want sharper stripe patterns, well-defined bed lines, and entry plantings that hold up to corner-lot visibility. The Township estates ask for the full toolkit: hardscape, drainage work, specimen tree installs, and the occasional retaining wall solving a real grade problem.

Most of our Northville clients have us doing more than mowing. We're working alongside Northville Public Schools families, around the trail systems, and on properties where the landscape matters as much as the kitchen renovation did. Twenty-five years on these streets means we know what shifts and what holds.

Whether you're in Edenderry, Carlton Estates, Innsbrook, Northridge, or right downtown off Cady or Main, you get the same foreman and the same standards.

Common questions

Things Northville homeowners usually ask.

Do you service historic downtown Northville and the Township?

Yes. We work in all three Northvilles: the historic downtown district, the luxury subdivisions like Northville Hills, Stonewater, and Country Club Village, and the larger lots out in Northville Township. The work is different in each one but we route through all of them.

How does Northville pricing compare to other cities?

Pricing tracks scope and access. Historic district lots tend to be smaller but require careful trim work around mature plantings. Subdivisions like Stonewater and Northville Hills are larger lots with more visible front entries. Township estates are bigger again. We give you a detailed line-item proposal so you know exactly what you're paying for.

Can you handle Northville's mature trees and historic homes?

Yes. The historic district has some of the oldest tree canopies in the area and that changes how we work: smaller equipment for tight access, careful trim work around heritage plantings, and planting palettes that respect the architecture. We've been doing it long enough to know what fits.

Do you offer year-round service in Northville?

Yes. Same crew handles spring through fall maintenance, hardscape and design installs, and winter snow and ice management for Northville homes, subdivisions, and commercial properties along Seven Mile and Center Street.

What's your snow response time in Northville?

Northville residential routes are typically cleared within a few hours of snowfall ending. Commercial accounts in the downtown corridor and along Seven Mile are pre-treated and monitored in real time and cleared before business hours.

Are you insured for Northville HOAs and condo associations?

Yes. We carry general liability and workers' compensation and provide certificates of insurance directly to property managers and HOA boards. Several Northville associations are on our route.

How quickly can you start service?

For weekly maintenance in Northville we can usually slot a new property within one to two weeks once you sign off on the proposal. For hardscape and design builds, lead times depend on season and scope, but we'll give you a real start date in the proposal.
