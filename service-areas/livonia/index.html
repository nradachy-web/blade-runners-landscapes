Our Hometown

Why Livonia calls us

Livonia is where this company was born and it's still where most of our crew lives. That changes how the work gets done.

We grew up cutting lawns on Five Mile, Six Mile, and Seven Mile. Our route still threads through Rosedale Gardens, Coventry Gardens, Burton Hollow, Castle Gardens, and Bonaparte Estates every week, and we maintain properties for families whose kids grew up at Stevenson, Churchill, and Franklin. When a Livonia homeowner calls us, they're usually a neighbor of someone we already work for.

The character of the city shapes how we work here. Livonia has some of the best mature tree canopies in metro Detroit (oaks, maples, ash that survived) and some of the heaviest clay sitting underneath. Brick ranches and colonials, established beds, school families, tight cul-de-sacs. The lawns here have history. They want a steady hand, not a chain that rotates crews every quarter.

That's the gap we fill. We are local enough that the foreman remembers your dog's name, and we run a tight enough operation that you never have to chase us about whether the crew is coming Thursday. Twenty-five years on the same streets builds a kind of muscle memory you can feel on a property the second you pull up.

Whether your property is a bungalow off Newburgh, a colonial in Greenwood, or one of the larger lots backing up to Hines Park, we treat it like our own block, because half the time, it is.

Common questions

Things Livonia homeowners usually ask.

Do you service my Livonia neighborhood?

Almost certainly yes. Our weekly route covers Rosedale Gardens, Coventry Gardens, Burton Hollow, Castle Gardens, Bonaparte Estates, Greenwood, Quaker Acres, Mayflower Heights, Devonshire, and the streets around Bicentennial Park. If you're somewhere else in Livonia, give us your address and we'll tell you which crew day you'd land on.

How does pricing in Livonia compare to other cities?

Livonia is the most efficient route on our schedule because it's our home city, so we tend to be sharper on weekly maintenance pricing here than the same square footage would cost in Novi or Northville. Hardscape and design pricing is the same standard regardless of city: it's based on scope, material grade, and site conditions, not zip code.

Can you handle Livonia's mature trees and clay soil?

That's most of what we do here. Livonia's older neighborhoods have some of the best tree canopies in the area and some of the heaviest clay underneath. We adjust our base prep for hardscape (extra aggregate, geotextile fabric where it's warranted), our planting palette (varieties that tolerate clay and root competition from oaks and maples), and our cleanup schedule for the leaf load every fall.

Do you offer year-round service in Livonia?

Yes. Lawn care from spring start-up through fall cleanup, then the same crew handles snow and ice management for residential driveways and commercial lots all winter. One company, one phone number, every season.

What's your snow response time in Livonia?

Commercial contracts in Livonia get pre-treated and monitored in real time during a storm, with priority lots cleared before business hours. Residential routes are typically cleared within a few hours of snowfall ending. Our trucks are based in Livonia, so we're often out before companies routing in from elsewhere.

Are you insured for HOAs and condo associations in Livonia?

Yes. Blade Runners Lawn & Landscapes, LLC carries general liability and workers' compensation, and we're set up to provide certificates of insurance directly to property managers and HOA boards. Several Livonia condo communities and HOA common areas are on our route.

How quickly can you start service?

For weekly maintenance in Livonia we can usually slot a new property within one to two weeks once you sign off on the proposal. For hardscape and design builds, lead times depend on season and scope, but we'll give you a real start date in the proposal, not a maybe.
