Common questions

Things people usually ask.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Livonia, Novi, Northville, Plymouth, Farmington Hills, Westland, Redford, Canton, South Lyon, and the surrounding communities on metro Detroit's west side. Outside our standard route? Call us, if we can make it work, we will.

Do you handle residential and commercial properties?

Both. About half our work is custom residential, full property care for homes that take landscaping seriously. The other half is commercial: office parks, retail centers, medical buildings, multifamily, and HOA common areas with seasonal contracts.

Are you the same crew year-round?

Yes. The crew that mows your lawn in July is the same one plowing your driveway in January. We do not subcontract our route work, which is a big reason our clients stay with us for ten, fifteen, twenty years.

How much does a paver patio cost in Michigan?

Most residential paver patios fall between $12,000 and $45,000 depending on size, paver grade, site prep, drainage work, and design complexity (steps, walls, fire features add cost). We provide free on-site estimates with detailed line-item pricing, no mystery numbers.

How fast is the response after a snowstorm?

Commercial contracts get pre-treated and monitored in real time during a storm, with priority lots cleared before business hours. Residential routes are typically cleared within a few hours of snowfall ending, earlier when we can.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Blade Runners Lawn & Landscapes, LLC is fully licensed and insured in the state of Michigan, including general liability and workers' comp. Certificates available on request, many of our commercial clients ask, and that's expected.

How do estimates work?

You call or send a message. We schedule a walk-through of your property, usually within a few days. We take measurements, talk through what you actually want (and what you don't), and send you a detailed written scope and price within about 48 hours. No high-pressure pitch.
